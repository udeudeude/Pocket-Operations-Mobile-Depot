Restores an existing PO-35-compatible backup. Does not synthesize backup data.

On the PO-35
  1. Use a normal stereo 3.5 mm cable to the left/input socket.
  2. Press RECORD + SOUND to enter receive mode.
  3. Press Play on the iPhone player above. Keep the screen awake until it finishes.

Start with iPhone output volume around 75%, then adjust if the PO-35 does not receive the data. Restoring may overwrite current sounds and patterns. Make a verified backup first if that state matters.

04

Cables & limitations

Check the audio route before trusting a transfer.

What that Apple adapter does

Its 3.5 mm socket can deliver stereo headphone output. With a compatible CTIA headset splitter, its microphone path carries mono audio. A regular TRS cable plugged directly into the adapter is not a reliable microphone-input connection.

What the browser does

Playback uses the original selected WAV file rather than re-encoding to MP3 or AAC. Capture requests echo cancellation, noise suppression and automatic gain control OFF, but iOS may ignore those requests. Recording writes a 16-bit PCM WAV at the actual Web Audio sample rate.

About full backup reliability

Teenage Engineering specifies stereo 16-bit/44.1 kHz or higher for full PO-35 backups. Stereo input generally requires another recording interface. Mono headset capture here is a diagnostic experiment, not a verified preservation workflow.
